About This Portal

The portal provides a centralized system for tracking applications, reviewing statuses, and managing landowner information. It is designed to improve transparency and efficiency in the application process under the PNG Mining Act 1992 (Form 5 & Form 8/17).

How to Use

  • Go to the Dashboard to view application statistics and recent submissions.
  • Visit the Landowner Form page or click + Submit AML Application to initiate an application online.
  • Check the Help page for guidance and FAQs.
